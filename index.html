Your ads × your flow

Your ads sell a comeback, a song you love and ten minutes a day. So we built three versions, one per promise.

The Yousician page in the Meta Ad Library shows 51 active ads on 11 November 2026, all started on 10 or 11 November for Black Friday. They make three promises: pick the guitar back up (“Remember the guitar you put down at 16? This time it sticks.”), learn a song you love (“No more tutorial videos please 😩”), and short lessons for people short on time. Only the comeback video links to the App Store; the other two run to Google Play and yousician.com. Whichever promise people saw, your sign-up asks the same four questions and ends on the same trial screen.

Flow Builder can open a different flow by the campaign an install came from. The three phones below are three versions of your sign-up: each skips what its ad already said, opens on what it promised, and ends on its own paywall, with three recommendations under each.

Pass the Meta campaign into Adapty

Week 1

Three versions, one per promise

The comeback, song and short-on-time ads each open their own version; the two web-only videos share the comeback one.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that ad earns.

All 51 ads are live in the Meta Ad Library with the texts above; only the comeback video links to the App Store today, so versions B and C show the iOS flow those promises could open. The Black Friday discounts in the ads (50–70%) are not in your App Store price list, so every paywall here uses your standing prices.
